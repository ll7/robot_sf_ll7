      aggregate evidence alone.
    never_replace: >-
      An example is never replaced because its picture disappoints. The first accepted
      seed in the committed screening order is the example; a rerun that differs from its
      screening row is reported as it is and not swapped for another seed.
    cell_rule: >-
      Among the claim's group cells, the eligible cells are those with at least
      min_matching_pairs source pairs that match every arm's class on the same seed. The
      chosen cell is the eligible cell whose paired-pattern frequency (matching pairs over
      complete pairs) is closest to the group frequency (all matching pairs over all
      complete pairs of the group); ties go to the smaller cell id in byte order.
    bands: >-
      Frozen per arm over the chosen cell's matching source pairs, 25th to 75th percentile
      with linear interpolation: minimum clearance (minimum robot-pedestrian separation
      minus the 1.4 m envelope radius sum; absent when no pedestrian was present), exposure
      steps (metrics.near_misses, the close-clearance exposure-step count) and time to goal
      (steps times the run time step, successes only). A measure the source does not
      record has no band and is not checked.
    acceptance: >-
      A screened seed is accepted when every arm's episode matches its class and, for a
      typical claim, every banded measure lies inside its band (inclusive). A boundary
      claim checks the class only and prints the bands as context.

      The seed band sorted by SHA-256 of "<selection_rng_seed>:<seed>"; screening runs take
      the seeds in this order and stop at the first accepted one or at the budget.

acquisition_spec_verbatim: |
  ## Common steps

  1. **Screening runs.** For the claim's chosen cell, run every listed arm on the seeds
     of the printed screening order, one seed at a time, in that order. Stop at the first
     accepted seed (rules.acceptance) or when the budget is spent. Screening runs record
     episode rows only; no trace is needed.
  2. **Screening ledger, kept in full.** One row per screened (seed, arm), including the
     rejected ones: claim id, screening index, seed, arm, scenario id, execution commit,
     config hash, episode row SHA-256, metrics.success, outcome.collision_event,
     outcome.timeout_event, termination_reason, steps, run_dt, ped_collision_count,
     obstacle_collision_count, wall_collisions, near_misses, the minimum pedestrian
     separation, the verdict per class predicate and per band, and the accepted flag.
     The ledger is committed with the packet; a seed with no row was not screened.
  3. **Trace-capable rerun.** Rerun the accepted seed for every arm with
     record_simulation_step_trace on (and the planner decision trace only where it does
     not change behaviour). Accept the trace only if its episode row equals the
     screening row on outcome, termination reason, steps, collision counts, near_misses
     and the minimum separation; otherwise report the rerun as it is (rules.never_replace).
  4. **Reproduction and pinning.** Commit a tracked packet in robot_sf_ll7 with the
     protocol claim id, the screening ledger, configs, the trace files and SHA256SUMS;
     add a docs/context/evidence_pins.yaml entry (commit and paths verified against the
     clone); route the figure through the figure-brief and admission workflow. Captions
     quote the base rates printed by the claim-keyed report for the chosen cell, never
     a rate read off the trace.
